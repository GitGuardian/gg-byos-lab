# GitGuardian BYOS Integration Hub

**Bring Your Own Source (BYOS) Examples and Integrations**

![BYOS Integration Hub Banner](./assets/bring-your-own-source-thumbnail.png)

Welcome to the BYOS Integration Hub! This repository showcases practical examples and integrations for GitGuardian's Bring Your Own Source feature, helping you scan any custom data source for exposed secrets and credentials.

### 👋 **What is BYOS?**

BYOS (Bring Your Own Source) empowers you to scan **any of your sources** for hardcoded secrets, even those not natively supported by GitGuardian integrations. 

**Can't find your native integration? Bring your own!**

### 🛡️ **Why use BYOS?**

GitGuardian BYOS allows you to:

- **Extend secret detection** to custom applications, legacy systems, and unique workflows
- **Maintain comprehensive security coverage** across your entire infrastructure
- **Create incidents automatically** in your GitGuardian dashboard for tracking and remediation
- **Leverage 450+ secret detectors** on any text-based content

### 📚 **Getting Started**

1. **Set up BYOS** in your GitGuardian dashboard following our [official documentation](https://docs.gitguardian.com/internal-monitoring/integrate-sources/bring-your-own-sources)
2. **Explore our examples** in this repository
3. **Adapt the code** to your specific use case
4. **Start scanning** and securing your custom sources!
